# Audima Sway: community and reviewer notes

Compiled from web sources, late November 2026. Independent user material is thin. Most of what exists is launch-era press, the vendor's own posts, and a vendor-hosted Trustpilot page. Labels: [User], [Reviewer], [Vendor]. Dates are publication or post dates.

## 1. Front-panel operation

- [Reviewer] 6 preset banks, each opened by press-and-hold; 3.2-inch OLED with a dedicated encoder; 8 further encoders plus a primary encoder; 16 pads and 8 buttons; 80 LEDs. Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026, https://magneticmag.com/2026/08/the-sway-by-audima-labs-could-be-the-most-innovative-midi-controller-of-2026/
- [Vendor] 36 custom presets managed in the desktop software (6 x 6). Bonedo, 12 Aug 2026, https://www.bonedo.de/?p=2830564
- [Reviewer] Pads behave like MPC-style pads; encoders feel secure. Same Magnetic Mag article.
- Not found in any source: dial pages A–H, what the 8 small buttons do, encoder/menu behaviour, button combos, the custom encoder/pad mapping toggle, brightness, low-power mode, calibration, reset.

## 2. Gestures, regions and playing

- [Reviewer] Modes are Strike (downstroke speed as velocity), Sway (left-right), Pulse (up-down, more for rhythmic modulation than notes), Glide (smooth movement without jumps), Press (in-out distance), Sculpt (two hands). Altwire, 16 Feb 2026, https://altwire.net/?p=21092398. Same review: X axis very responsive; Y detects roughly a foot above the surface, and lighting may affect range.
- [Reviewer] Modes are not obvious at first; the reviewer recommends Audima's YouTube guides (same Altwire review).
- [Reviewer] Zones are shown as LEDs in the space above the unit. Magnetic Mag author says it "appears to" not use skeletal hand tracking; this is his inference. Same Magnetic Mag article.
- [Reviewer] Learning curve for keyboard and pad players; playing can be physically tiring (Magnetic Mag; Altwire).
- [Reviewer] Gestures can trigger or shape sounds, e.g. air drum hits or filter sweeps (vendor-described; Bonedo).
- [User] One Trustpilot reviewer says latency "exceeded expectations" and calls it a revolutionary controller. D rock, 5 Aug 2026, https://fi.trustpilot.com/review/audima.com.au (Trustpilot, vendor-hosted listing; not independently checked).
- [User] Expressive and fun for non-keyboard players in Ableton, "some room for improvement". Dj Nomad, 15 Aug 2026, same Trustpilot page.
- [User] Accessibility/ergonomics: suggested for players with repetitive strain or joint problems (Phillip, 22 Nov 2025; m0ld, 22 Nov 2025). Ergonomic concern about elbow, shoulder and neck strain, suggesting Linnstrument or Continuum as lighter-touch options (g3o2, 23 Nov 2025). Elektronauts, https://www.elektronauts.com/t/audima-labs-sway-midi-controller/242226

## 3. Desktop app, firmware and updates

- [Reviewer] Sway Software manages layouts, chord/mapping setups, colours and presets, stored per project, genre or show. Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026.
- [Vendor] Updates added two-hand, two-note playing and expanded chord mapping; a DAW program-change feature was reportedly added (not verified by the reviewer). Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026; Bonedo, 12 Aug 2026.
- [User] A firmware update broke the display on one unit; support gave recovery steps, then shipped a replacement abroad. manuel roldan, 25 Oct 2026, Trustpilot page above.
- [Reviewer] A firmware and software update was scheduled about a month after the Altwire review (16 Feb 2026); the reviewer said a hands-on update would follow. Altwire, above. No release notes found.

## 4. DAW and hardware setups

- [Reviewer] Recognised by the companion software on macOS and Windows; Ableton Live connected via the provided remote script. MIDI remote scripts are said to exist for Ableton Live and Cubase. Altwire, 16 Feb 2026.
- [Reviewer] Tested with Logic Pro 12.3 and Ableton Live 12.4.3 on an M4 Pro Mac mini, with no connectivity problems, alongside a standard MIDI keyboard. Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026.
- [Vendor] DAW list covers Logic, Ableton, FL Studio and others on macOS, Windows and Linux over USB-C; DJ software named includes Traktor, Rekordbox, Serato and VirtualDJ. Vendor site as quoted in Magnetic Mag (not checked here).
- [Reviewer] One MIDI Out via a 3.5 mm TRS jack, so it can drive hardware without a computer. Dual-role USB-C plus a separate USB-C power port. Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026.
- [User] FL Studio: a Trustpilot reviewer tried it briefly and was impressed. Tom Shavit, 3 Sep 2026, Trustpilot page above.
- Not found: MIDI channels, CC numbers, MPE behaviour, Logic/Cubase/DJ-software workflows in detail.

## 5. Complaints, limits, support and batches

- [Reviewer] Price of USD 830 is well above a MIDI keyboard; the reviewer suggests cheaper options for budget producers. Magnetic Mag; Altwire ("premium-priced", batch 2 pricing); Bonedo lists the same price.
- [Reviewer] Not built for heavy abuse; small-batch hardware. Magnetic Mag; Altwire.
- [User] Hardware faults reported and resolved by repair or replacement: broke in use, repaired under warranty (Hans Mika, 12 Sep 2026); Batch 3 unit did not power on, replacement arranged (Mario, 19 Aug 2026); a technical fault, free replacement offered (Rachel, 15 Nov 2026). Trustpilot page above.
- [User] Support praised in about 10 of 17 Trustpilot reviews, including quick email help and handling of PayPal instalment problems. One reviewer notes there is no phone line (Alvaro Velarde, 2 Sep 2026).
- [User] Shipping and batch progress praised, with monthly order updates (Fabian Nakandakari, 5 Aug 2026; fil evo, 4 Aug 2026). Trustpilot page above.
- [Vendor] Batch 4 pre-orders accepted, deliveries expected Nov–Dec 2026; first batch sold out in seven minutes (Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026; Bonedo, 12 Aug 2026). Timing may change (Magnetic Mag).
- [Vendor] Crowdfunding total USD 220,958 against a USD 22,800 goal (Magnetic Mag, 7 Aug 2026).
- Feature requests: none found from users.
- Trustpilot shows 17 reviews, TrustScore 4.5 (vendor-hosted; treat as the vendor's own channel).
